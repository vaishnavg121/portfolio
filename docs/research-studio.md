# Research Studio

The Studio lives on the homepage at `#research-studio`. The comparison route at `/preview/research-studio` renders the homepage in local development and on deployments built with `RESEARCH_STUDIO_PREVIEW=1`.

## Editing the model

- `studio-plan.ts` defines the four bay coordinates, phase labels, and project links for the completed stations.
- `studio-architecture.ts` extrudes one common floor outline for the full cutaway. The perimeter bevel, structural layers, and continuous panel joints share that shape. Slim service channels sit below the original station ports. The preview query `shell=A` shows the open shell and `shell=B` adds the deeper smoked-glass enclosure.
- `studio-model.ts` derives materials from portfolio tokens, rounds and batches opaque geometry, and creates the local contact shading and nameplates. Transparent panes stay separate for depth sorting.
- `studio-interior.ts` uses the shared rear trim, lower wall construction, window reveals, and a three-piece gallery. The `room=straight` comparison holds the furniture farther right on a rectangular floor. The default angled cut brings the desk closer to the research bays. Curtain vertices update at up to 12 Hz from a fixed-size analytic deformation.
- `studio-furniture.ts` batches the floor lamp, rug, desk, and gaming equipment. The rounded cushions have a shaped profile and modeled seams. The PC fans and seat response advance on the room clock. `studio-interior-media.ts` uses two original screen shaders and draws the landscape, architectural, and ink prints to small textures.
- `studio-light.ts` creates a small prefiltered environment map once, with a warm key light and restrained fill. The preview queries `light=soft` and `light=rim` show the alternative light rigs.
- `station-learning.ts` contains the sensor manifold, transparent chamber, encoder rotor, compute modules, forecast panes and conduit paths. Its data particles are instanced. The visible forecast shapes are illustrative and carry no measured values.
- `agentic-machinery.ts` models the archive, horizontal optical vault, distinct processing units, verification gate, and output tray. The hybrid combines a three-tier archive with a wider chamber. The preview queries `agentic=mechanical` and `agentic=optical` show the two alternative compositions.
- `station-agentic.ts` sets the timing, connects the machine ports with 3D curves, and animates the document plates, instanced query signals, clustered embeddings, and cited output layers. The query uses the nearest illustrative cluster. Swa.Neeti inspired the evidence check. The floor handoff represents a conceptual connection between disciplines.
- `finance-machinery.ts` models the Finance plinth, suspended analysis bed, graduated loss gimbal, three account lanes, and balance cradle. The queries `finance=research` and `finance=engineering` render the two initial compositions. The default combines the wide research surface with the compact engineered ledger.
- `markets-math.ts` defines the three illustrative calculations. `markets-surface.ts` shares a position buffer between the translucent surface and its wire grid. `station-finance.ts` drives the surface scan, loss distribution, and allocation gates with a 14-second standalone cycle. The guided pricing scenario uses the pricing and risk instruments.
- `robotics-arm.ts` builds the pedestal, beveled links, layered bearings, cable guides, wrist, and opposed fingers. `robotics-cell.ts` builds the controller, feeder, demonstration rover, and side-mounted optical carriage. The queries `robotics=assembly` and `robotics=computing` show the initial alternatives.
- `robotics-kinematics.ts` defines the arm geometry, joint limits, tool trajectory, and cassette handoffs. `station-robotics.ts` poses the rig, follows the gripper's world position, animates the inspection sweep, and routes the control and feedback signals.
- `studio-ports.ts` maps each workstation's local intake and outlet to the common floor rail. `studio-network.ts` builds the trench hardware, recessed junction, and physical collars. It caches arc-length samples, draws packets in two instanced pools, and reuses the same buffer for the trail. The trail, floor reflection, and port lamps follow the current transfer.
- `studio-choreography.ts` advances four independently offset station clocks even while a machine or personal object is selected. Route cues raise local emphasis and gate departures on the sending station’s existing output phase. Switching views leaves all station poses continuous.
- `studio-runtime.ts` owns the camera, single render loop, lifecycle, and resource disposal. It frames the whole room, a route, or an individual target. A pixel budget caps resolution as the viewport grows. Background machinery uses its lower update cadence. The renderer suspends outside the viewport and while the tab is hidden.
- `ResearchStudio.tsx` and `StudioConnections.tsx` expose selection and contextual links by pointer, touch, and keyboard. Desktop uses the projected object bounds to pick a stable panel gutter. The panel sits beneath the model on phones. The object list and still renders provide equivalent inspection controls when WebGL is unavailable. Reduced motion has a stationary room and route.

The fallback WebPs are renders of the same editable Three.js geometry. To regenerate them, open the preview with `still=1`, capture the transparent WebGL canvas in the overview and selected poses, and trim the transparent margin before exporting WebP.

The cutaway composition takes cues from the use of deliberate room boundaries and soft baked shadows in authored browser interiors. The curtains use vertex deformation to suggest fabric folds with a fixed geometry budget. ## Shared sequences

- **Data to intelligence** sends observations into the learning station and uses the resulting forecast as an illustrative research lead for retrieval and evidence checking.
- **Intelligence and finance** uses a model result as a hypothetical pricing input. The finance instrument reads the loss tail and reports back to the floor junction.
- **Computation and robotics** sends a command to the controller. The arm fits and inspects the cassette, then an agent organizes its inspection evidence as the arm resets.

The sequences illustrate connections between disciplines. `hub=0` shows the floor-only prototype and `mode=exhibit` plays sequences on request. The default combines the recessed junction, calm ambient playback, and optional exploration. The comparisons use matching cameras and the same workstation models.

- Routed packets use the Three.js distance-based curve interface. Separate transfer, processing, and rest intervals follow the sequencing and path techniques in the Blender and GSAP references. Guided inspection uses fixed overview and pair targets as in interactive 3D showcases. [Three.js Curve](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Curve.html), [Blender Follow Path](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/animation/constraints/relationship/follow_path.html), [GSAP Timeline](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/GSAP/Timeline/), and [Threepipe showcase](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2024/08/07/interactive-3d-device-showcase-with-threepipe/).

## Finance models and references

- The market grid maps spot price and time remaining to profit or loss for one share and one long put. The put uses a zero-rate, zero-dividend Black–Scholes model and a fixed illustrative cost basis. Changing the assumed volatility lifts or lowers the time-dependent curvature while expiry keeps the put's payoff floor. The sweeping trace follows a constant-time section of the mesh. [Protective put payoff](https://www.optionseducation.org/strategies/all-strategies/protective-put-married-put) and [Black and Scholes, 1973](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/260062).
- The independent risk instrument stacks samples of a standard normal loss density. Its upright marker is the 95th percentile of losses. A second marker locates the conditional mean in the upper 5% tail. [NIST normal density](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda3661.htm) and [BIS conference proceedings on VaR and Expected Shortfall](https://www.bis.org/cgfs/conf/mar02.pdf).
- The ledger represents an illustrative 1,200 minor-unit debit and three credits of 500, 400, and 300. The balance responds to the outstanding total, and the output is a conceptual signal in the shared Studio trench. [Spenza case study](https://vaishnav-gadamsetty.vercel.app/work/spenza).
- The circular fixture uses the scaled proportions of optical positioning hardware. The sculpted surface uses a shared indexed buffer instead of rebuilding its wire geometry. Conduits use fixed cross-sections with distance-sampled packets on the established WebGL renderer. [Thorlabs translation stages](https://www.thorlabs.com/Images/PDF/Vol18_226.pdf), [Blender curve geometry](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/geometry_nodes/curve/operations/curve_to_mesh.html), [Three.js buffer attributes](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/BufferAttribute.html), and [Codrops procedural geometry](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/08/11/exploring-procedural-geometry-with-three-js-and-webgpu/).
- The wire grid uses major lines in overview and adds subdivisions in focus. The contour examples informed the line hierarchy, and the pricing function supplies the surface's actual elevation. [CodePen parametric surfaces](https://codepen.io/wakana-k/pen/BaeypJL) and [Shadertoy contour example and source](https://github.com/alx-m24/Perlin-Noise-Contours).

## Systems & Robotics models and references

- The portfolio's Velociraptor GLB is a static engineering export with 660 meshes. The demonstration rover draws on the compact chassis silhouette and ThriveForce's documented CAD and engineering work. The station uses an editable procedural rig and a simplified sensor cassette. [ThriveForce](https://vaishnav-gadamsetty.vercel.app/#robotics) and [Blender glTF export guide](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/addons/import_export/scene_gltf2.html).
- The arm uses a rotating pedestal and a fixed elbow-up solution for the shoulder and elbow. The wrist keeps the fingers vertical and cancels pedestal yaw so the cassette arrives in the fixture's orientation. The gripper and both seats share exact handoff positions. The arm returns the cassette to the feeder after inspection. [Universal Robots joint reference](https://www.universal-robots.com/manuals/EN/PDF/SW5_24/service-manual_e-series/e-Series_Service_Manual_en.pdf), [Modern Robotics inverse kinematics](https://modernrobotics.northwestern.edu/nu-gm-book-resource/6-1-1-inverse-kinematics-of-open-chains/), and [Three.js object transforms](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Object3D.html).
- The side-mounted carriage sweeps a narrow plane across the assembly and reveals an outline of the rover and cassette. The sequence illustrates optical inspection and feedback. The visual treatment uses one shared light rig, static contact shading, and distance-sampled pulses along the physical conduits. [KEYENCE line profiling](https://www.keyence.com/products/measure/laser-2d/) and [Codrops Three.js motion case study](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2022/04/25/case-study-windland-an-immersive-three-js-experience/).
